Formula

Luminous intensity from raw spectral data, using the CIE 1931 V(λ) photopic luminosity function and a machine-calibrated constant K.
Iv [mcd] = K × Σ P(λ) · V(λ) · Δλ
Iv — Luminous Intensity (mcd, Mean Spherical Candela)  ·  K — machine calibration constant  ·  P(λ) — raw spectral intensity  ·  V(λ) — CIE 1931 photopic luminosity function (1nm, 380–780nm)  ·  Δλ — wavelength step  ·  integrated by the trapezoidal rule.

Machine constant K & integration range

K = Km × (1000/4π) × C_radiometric — instrument-specific, must be (re-)calibrated whenever the detector changes. This K is shared: it's also used for the Iv shown on the Train/Test tabs.
Km (683 lm/W, fixed) × 1000/4π (79.5775, integrating-sphere geometry) × C_radiometric (instrument-specific).
